---
title: Overview of V8 heap management
speaker: Caio Lima
speakerId: caio-lima
---

In this talk I'd like to share some knowledge I gathered during the
implementation of an allocation counter about how V8 Heap is organized,
including GC algorithms, Heap Layout, mutator integration with GC, and some
ways to inspect the heap in a safe way while taking in consideration V8's
concurrency behavior.
