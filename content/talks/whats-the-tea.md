A Node.js team you've probably never heard of is "next-10": we keep our ears
to the ground, eyes on the sky, and fingers on the pulse. But what does that
mean? Our mandate is to guide the project forward, ensuring we're delivering
the features users want and also maintaining a healthy contributor
environment.

To do that, we regularly check in with project maintainers, contributors, and
end-users. This year, we uncovered some particularly interesting new trends
and tidbits, as well as some long-standing issues that came to an (explosive)
head.

We'll discuss those nuggets and landmines, and talk about what we're doing
about them. Some highlights include:

- new geographic "markets" for Node
- popular experiments
- tasty features
- maintainership
- AI
