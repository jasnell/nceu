---
title: The architecture of a massive multi-tenant storage system with Node.js at Supabase
speaker: Fabrizio Fenoglio
speakerId: fabrizio-fenoglio
---

A deep dive into one of the biggest multi-tenant systems ever built with
Node.js, powering millions of uploads and requests per day at Supabase.

We'll explore the architecture of that system — how tenants are isolated, how
uploads and reads are routed, and where the hard edges turned out to be — and
how it was scaled over time as the workload grew.
