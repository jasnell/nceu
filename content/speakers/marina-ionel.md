Marina is a Frontend Tech Lead at UNIwise, where she leads the team behind
WISEflow's frontend. She led its migration from a mix of jQuery, AngularJS, and
React into a fleet of independently deployed React microfrontends, standardized
on a single Node-based build stack.

Now she spends most of her time arguing that tooling investment is a
feature-delivery strategy, not a tax on it — and, more to the point, proving it
with numbers.
