Filip is an open-source maintainer who implements and contributes to Internet
Standards across identity, cryptography, and JavaScript runtimes. He maintains
widely used libraries for OpenID Connect, OAuth 2.0, JOSE, JWT, JWK, and HPKE,
and contributes to standards processes at the IETF, OpenID Foundation, and
W3C. He is a Node.js core collaborator and Technical Steering Committee
member, focusing on `node:crypto` and Node.js' Web Cryptography API
implementation.
