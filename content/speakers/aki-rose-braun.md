Aki Rose is an engineer and consultant frequently shepherding technical
standards. She has nearly three decades of programming experience in a wide
range of industries, with a focus on free and open-source software and
industry's relationship with it. Aki is the special rapporteur for the
harmonised EU standard for VPN cybersecurity conformance with the Cyber
Resilience Act, and an active contributor to several additional CRA standards.
