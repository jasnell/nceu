Fabrizio is the lead storage engineer at Supabase, where he builds and scales
the multi-tenant storage system behind millions of uploads and requests a day.
His background is in distributed systems and media processing and delivery, and
he is a long-time Node.js and Go engineer.

He is happiest inside highly scalable architectures and distributed storage
systems — the kind that have to keep working while the workload underneath them
grows by an order of magnitude.
