David Mark Clements has been writing JavaScript since 1996. A Node.js
contributor since version 0.8 (2012), he's the original author of *Node
Cookbook* and led the OpenJS Node.js Certifications program, helping shape how
the industry trains and validates backend engineers at scale.

Today David is driving one of the most ambitious bets in modern infrastructure:
peer-to-peer computing as a production-ready paradigm.
