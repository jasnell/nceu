Rafael is a Principal Open Source Engineer at NodeSource and a member of the
Node.js Technical Steering Committee, where he works full-time on the Node.js
runtime with a focus on security and performance. His work spans runtime
internals, benchmarking, and secure system design, with contributions to
widely used tools in the Node.js ecosystem.
