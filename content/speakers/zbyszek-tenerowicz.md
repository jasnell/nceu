JavaScript and Web enthusiast, pushing web security to the limit building
LavaMoat and Endo. Zbyszek started using Node.js at v0.8 and never stopped. He
enjoys innovating and teaching security, diagnostics, and maintainability, is
one of the oldest members of the meet.js Poland community — both as a speaker
and organizer — and, most recently, a TC39 noobie.
