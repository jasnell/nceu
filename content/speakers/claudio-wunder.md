Claudio is a Platform Engineer at HubSpot, where he helps maintain part of the
frontend infrastructure. He is also a Node.js core collaborator working on the
project's developer experience and infrastructure, co-chair of webpack's
Technical Steering Committee, and a voting member of the OpenJS Foundation. He
previously contributed to the GNOME Foundation.
