Yagiz is a member of the Node.js Technical Steering Committee and a Member of
Technical Staff at xAI. Most of his open-source work is about making Node.js
faster where it counts: he wrote the Ada URL parser that now powers Node's URL
implementation, and has spent the past several months cutting per-request and
per-header overhead out of the HTTP server path in core.

He is a firm believer in measuring before optimizing, and in shipping
performance wins without asking anyone to change a line of their code.
