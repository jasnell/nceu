Mikola is a Principal Software Engineer at Socket, where his work focuses on
software supply chain security: detecting malicious packages and fixing
vulnerable ones. An early and prolific contributor to the JavaScript
open-source ecosystem, he created `scijs/ndarray`, the de facto
multidimensional-array primitive for numerical computing in JavaScript, and
helped build the stack.gl ecosystem of composable WebGL modules. His earlier
work spans graphics, computational geometry, and data visualization, and his
blog 0fps.net is now classic reference material for graphics and game
developers.
