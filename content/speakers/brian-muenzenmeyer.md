Brian is a Principal Engineer active in the open source community. A father of
four boys, he juggles roles like developer, bald curmudgeon, and coffee
drinker. Brian maintains the Node.js website, organizes Grace Hopper
hackathons, and wrote *Approachable Open Source*, the missing manual for
newcomers and veterans alike. He enjoys woodworking, soccer, milkshakes, and
pickleball.
